About Me

Writing is my Core Identity

Some express themselves through speech; I do it through words. For over four years, I have written features for national dailies, shaping stories of human lives. Writing is my passion, which led me to leadership at the Bangladesh Young Column Writers Forum.

Technical skills have now merged with this creative journey. Currently, I serve as the Director of the Fulbaria ICT Training Institute and am a Certified Trainer and Assessor under the National Skills Development Authority (NSDA) and the Bangladesh Technical Education Board (BTEB).

My goal is simple—to let my writing provoke thought and my teachings empower youth to become self-reliant. I am Tanjid—an equation of story and speed.
