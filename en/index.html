About me

Writing is my core identity

Some people express themselves by speaking. I do it with words. For more than seven years I have written features for national dailies and shaped the stories of ordinary lives. Writing is my habit and my passion, and it led me to a leadership role at the Bangladesh Young Column Writers Forum.

Technical skills have since joined that creative path. I am the Director of the Fulbaria ICT Training Institute and a certified Trainer and Assessor under the National Skills Development Authority (NSDA) and the Bangladesh Technical Education Board (BTEB). My goal is simple: just as my writing makes people think, my training should help young people stand on their own feet.

I started with words and expanded into technology. One hand writes features, the other helps build skilled professionals. I am Tanjid, an odd equation of story and speed.
